# End User Guide

This guide explains how to use the Academic Portal as a visitor, student, or applicant.

---

## Overview

The Academic Portal is the public website for the institution. You can:

- Browse courses and academic information
- Read notices and announcements
- Search exam results and verify student details
- Access examination forms, FAQs, and grading information
- Submit the contact form
- Use student services (verification, application forms, fee payment info)
- Log in (if you have an account) to access your dashboard and profile

---

## Home Page

The **home page** is the main landing page. From here you can:

- Use the **top bar** for quick links (e.g. PayU, social media) if they are set up
- Use the **header** to go to **Academic**, **Notices**, and other sections from the main menu
- Use the **footer** for contact details, social links, and extra navigation

Navigation items and some labels are configurable by the institution, so menus may vary by site.

---

## Notices & Announcements

- **Notices list**: Open **Notices** from the menu to see all published notices.
- **Single notice**: Click a notice to read the full content. Notice links may also appear in the announcement ticker on the home page.

---

## Academic (Courses)

- **Academic** in the menu takes you to the academic/courses section.
- You can browse **course categories** and **individual courses**.
- From a course page you can **download the syllabus** if a file is available.

### How the academic terms relate (Course Categories, Courses, Specializations, Subjects, Syllabi)

These terms form a hierarchy and serve different purposes on the portal.

| Term | What it is | Where you see it | Use case |
|------|------------|------------------|----------|
| **Course Category** | A top-level grouping of programs (e.g. “Undergraduate”, “Postgraduate”, “Diploma”). | Academic home and main menu: you pick a category to see its courses. | Browsing: “I want to see all UG courses.” |
| **Course** | A specific program (e.g. “B.Tech Computer Science”, “MBA”). It has duration, intake, eligibility, description. | Listed under a category; each has its own course page. | Choosing a program: “I want details of B.Tech CSE.” |
| **Specialization** | An optional track or focus *within* a course (e.g. “AI & ML”, “Data Science” under B.Tech CSE). | On the **course page**: list of specializations with description, industry relevance, career outcomes. | Understanding options: “What focus areas does this course offer?” |
| **Subject** | A single paper/unit in a course (e.g. “Mathematics I”, “Programming”). Has a code, max marks, passing marks. | **Not shown on the public site.** Used internally for exam sessions and result calculation. | Back-office: defining which subjects are examined and how marks are stored. |
| **Syllabus** | The official syllabus document (usually a PDF) for a course (and optionally for a specialization), for a given academic year/version. | On the **course page**: “Syllabus” section with one or more download links (e.g. by year or version). | Downloading: “I need the syllabus PDF for this course.” |

**Relationships (simplified):**

- **Course Category** → has many **Courses**.
- **Course** → has many **Specializations**, many **Subjects**, and many **Syllabi** (one active per course or per course+specialization at a time).
- **Syllabus** → belongs to one **Course** and optionally to one **Specialization** (so you can have a syllabus for “B.Tech CSE” in general or for “B.Tech CSE – AI & ML”).
- **Subject** → belongs to one **Course**; used when building exam sessions and recording marks, not for public browsing.

So as an **end user** you mainly work with: **Course Category** → **Course** → (optional) **Specialization** and **Syllabus** downloads. **Subjects** are used behind the scenes for exams and results.

## Results

- Go to **Results** (from the menu or the results link on the site).
- Enter your **Roll Number** or **Enrollment ID** in the search box.
- Click search to see your **exam results by session** (if you are a registered student with published results).

If no record is found, the page will indicate that no result is available for the entered details.

---

## Placements

- **Recruiters**: View the list of recruiters/companies.
- **Statistics**: View placement statistics (if published).

---

## Contact

- Go to **Contact** from the menu.
- Fill in **Name**, **Email**, **Phone** (optional), **Subject**, and **Message**.
- Click **Send Message**. You should see a confirmation that your message was received. The institution will respond using the contact details they maintain.

---

## Examination

Examination-related pages are under **Examination** (or equivalent menu label):

| Page / action | What you can do |
|----------------|------------------|
| **Forms** | See a list of exam-related forms and **download** the ones you need. |
| **Center** | Read information about the examination center. |
| **FAQs** | Read frequently asked questions about examinations. |
| **Grading system** | View the grading rules used for exams. |

Download links on the Forms page will open or download the file (e.g. PDF) for that form.

---

## Student Corner

Student-focused pages (often under **Students** or **Student Corner**):

### Student verification

- Go to **Student Verification**.
- Enter your **Roll Number** or **Enrollment ID** and search.
- If found, your **student details** (e.g. name, course) are shown so employers or others can verify your enrollment.

### Application forms

- Go to **Application Forms** (or equivalent).
- You will see a list of **admission-related forms** available for download. Use the download link for each form you need.

### Pay fee

- Go to **Pay Fee** (or the link in the menu/footer).
- This page shows the institution’s **fee payment instructions** and any payment link (e.g. PayU) if configured. Follow the instructions on that page.

---

## Other pages (About, Director’s message, etc.)

The site may include pages such as:

- **About**
- **Director’s message**
- **Vision & Mission**
- **Notices** (as a page)
- **Pay Fee** (as a page)

These are linked from the main menu or footer. Open them like any other page to read the content.

---

## Logging in

- If the institution uses **central login**, you may be redirected to a central login page when you try to sign in.
- Use the **Login** (or **Sign in**) link in the header to log in.
- After logging in:
  - **Students**: You may have access to a **Student dashboard** (e.g. **Student** or **Dashboard** in the menu).
  - **Other roles**: You may be taken to the **admin dashboard** if you have staff/admin access.
- Use **Profile** to update your account details (e.g. name, email, password).
- Use **Logout** to sign out.

---

## PayU and payments

If the institution has set up **PayU** (or another payment link):

- A **PayU** (or “Pay now”) link may appear in the **top bar** of the site.
- The **Pay Fee** page (under Student Corner or menu) may also describe how to pay fees and may link to the same payment gateway.
- Always follow the instructions on the Pay Fee page and use only the official links provided by the institution.

---

## Tips

- Use **Roll Number** or **Enrollment ID** exactly as on your identity card or admission letter when searching **Results** or **Student Verification**.
- If a form or download does not open, check your browser’s download folder or allow pop-ups/downloads for the site if required.
- For any issue with results, verification, or payments, contact the institution using the **Contact** form or the phone/email shown in the header or footer.

---

*This guide describes the standard features of the Academic Portal. Your institution may enable or hide some sections; follow the links and labels on your site for the exact options available.*
